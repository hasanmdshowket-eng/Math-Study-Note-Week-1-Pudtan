1. Introduction to Whole Numbers

A whole number (จำนวนนับ) is any of the counting numbers 0, 1, 2, 3... In Grade 5, we extend our mathematical work far beyond 1,000,000. We are stepping up to the hundred-millions place and beyond!

4. Reading Large Numbers

In English

  • Step 1: Group digits in 3s from the right using commas: 47236158 → 47,236,158
  • Step 2: Read each group, then say its 'family name' (million / thousand).
  • Step 3: The last group (ones) is read without a family name.

In Thai

Thai groups by หลัก: หน่วย-สิบ-ร้อย-พัน-หมื่น-แสน-ล้าน-สิบล้าน-ร้อยล้าน

Example: 47,236,158 → "สี่สิบเจ็ดล้านสองแสนสามหมื่นหกพันหนึ่งร้อยห้าสิบแปด"

6. Review & Summary

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Forgetting to place commas every 3 digits.
  • Confusing 'place value' with 'value of a digit'.
  • Saying 'and' between groups in formal English.
  • Skipping zeros in word form.
